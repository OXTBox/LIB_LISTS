 A large, heavily-muscled (STR 18/00) brick-wall of a warrior arrives as part of the guard detail for a wealthy merchant. While no one even *thinks* of going near the merchant during the daytime, this imposing figure spends his nights at the tavern wearing only his breeches, showing off his massive frame covered in tattoos. His skin is tanned, and the tattoos on his body are unlike anything anyone in the region is familiar with. Every night that this monstrous man is in the tavern, he drinks more than 4 normal men and proceeds to find his way into a tavern-clearing brawl. The captain of the guard does not wish to offend the very lucrative trade that the merchant brings to the Keep, so he asks the PCs if they might help 'deal' with this tattooed menace .
