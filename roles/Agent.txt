 A snively weasel of a man arrives in the Keep to do some gem-trading. His knowledge of gems and their properties/values is second to none. He walks with a slight limp or a full-on hobble, depending on how much walking he has to do that day. He offers to purchase most gems of 50gp value or higher for up to 60% of their value, making him an instant threat to the other gem traders and guild members. The Guildhouse will take a neutral stance on the Agent's trading activities, as he has only paid dues for two weeks, after which time he has flatly stated he must move on. Still, his presence might encourage other guilders to seek out some freelance operatives to encourage an earlier departure for the upstart. In reality, the Agent works for the powers that control the Caves of the Unknown, and he is in town to assess the strength of the garrison. Within the week after the Agent's departure, a group of thieves will break into the Loan Bank (area 11) and steal a large amount of the valuables within. The Agent himself doesn't appear to ever drink alcohol or engage in any form of gambling or womanizing. Unless discussing a gem trade, his personality is icy, sour, and generally makes people want to be elsewhere
