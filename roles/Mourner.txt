 A cad, a ne'er-do-well, a prostitute, a con artist... but undeniably handsome and charming. Will attend a funeral and cry on cue for 1 GP (hence his name), but offers other services as well. Destroying a marriage through seduction (200 GP flat fee), spreading rumors about a rival (5 GP a day), provoking a mild-mannered person into a duel (100 GP) and much more. A master manipulator and social assassin who destroys reputations without any physical violence.
