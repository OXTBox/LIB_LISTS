 This charming man (CHA 18) is a professional courier and negotiator for the Guild (Keep #16). He has been summoned to the Keep to possibly negotiate a ransom for the "Plump, half-dead merchant" (Caves # 24). He does not trust the pampered and untested Guild men-at-arms, and his contract gives him the authority to hire local talent (albeit for a measly 7 GP a day, but he will let the PCs take any treasure if he gets the Guild's reward money).
