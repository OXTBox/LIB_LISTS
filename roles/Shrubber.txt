 Traveling artisan of flora. Bastion of morals. Savior of old ladies. The considerable economic stress of the REALM in this period in history has driven Roger the Shrubber to take his trade on the road. He has a cart full of tools and various types of shrubbery, both flowering (annual and perennial) and non-flowering varieties. Prone to ranting about pestilence abroad in the land. Cart is pulled by a horse named Bruce
