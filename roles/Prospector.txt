 A half mad old dwarf who is convinced that there is a major gold mine somewhere around the keep. He is usually around the keep every month or so to buy supplies, but always draws a lot of attention to himself whenever he is in town, especially as he been running low on funds and trying to find an investor to finance his further expeditions. While he can be fun and friendly, especially when drinking in town, he is paranoid, violent, and greedy, always willing to assume that adventurers he meets in the wild are claim jumpers deserving a good crossbow to the face, and if he was traveling with a party when he finds gold, he might be willing to betray his partners and keep the gold for himself, by any means necessary
