 This ancient, wandering junkman and his hot, mute, jailbait daughter have parked their rickety cart in an unused corner of the Entry Yard. He will buy weapons and armor scavenged from the monsters of the Caves at 1/3rd the new cost. If the PCs desire some odd item not for sale in the Keep, assume a base 1 in 6 chance that he has one (although probably not in the best condition)
