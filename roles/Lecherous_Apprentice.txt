 A runaway apprentice mage has been hiding at the Keep disguised as a simple traveler for the past week. His features are plain and somewhat haggard. He is on the run from his tutor after stealing a powerful magic item, a Wand of Charm Person (will have 22 charges remaining at the time of contact). He has used this wand to woo several female travelers, merchant's daughters, and heavy-pursed merchants to fuel his 'appetites for companionship' and to pay his way at the inn. The Innkeeper has begun to notice that things are not right, but the Corporal of the Watch has somehow 'befriended' the apprentice, and will not hear of anyone slandering his friend .
