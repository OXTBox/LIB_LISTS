 17 years old. The fourth prince in line to the throne of a minor Chaotic nation, being held at the Keep as a prisoner as one of the conditions of a complex peace treaty. Spoiled and arrogant, but intelligent, pragmatic, and no pushover. Is forbidden from carrying weapons or leaving the Keep, but is otherwise free to move about. Pampered by the staff of the Keep, The Hostage is in no hurry to leave (he is secretly having a passionate affair with the much older tutor teaching him Common), but if things at the Keep start to go to Hell (Siege, plague, rebellion, etc.) he may approach morally ambiguous PCs for help with an escape, offering them knighthoods in his homeland if they can get him out and over the border.
