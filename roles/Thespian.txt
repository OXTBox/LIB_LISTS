 A well-known actress from the REALM pays a visit to the keep with her troupe and their protective entourage (no less than six level 2 Fighters). They will occupy both private quarters (7a and 7b) for their week-long stay, the troupe in one apartment, and the guards in the other. Throughout the week, the troupe will put on various performances around the keep common areas, but the Thespian is definitely the star of the show. An extremely comely lass of 19 summers, she is used to the fawning adoration of all types of fans, which she warmly rebuffs. Does she have a stalking, rabin fan? Their last performance before departing takes place in the Inner Bailey, with the full permission and support of the Castellan, who is enamored with the Thespian and will attempt to woo her all throughout her stay. During the latter parts of the week, the population of the keep will increase 20-30%, as those from the lands west and southwest of the Keep learn of the rare opportunity for unique entertainment. The last performance can be a good distraction for those seeking opportunities for less-than-honorable activities, or, conversely, the Thespian could turn out to be some form of double-agent who arranges for the murder of the Castellan during the height of the show .
