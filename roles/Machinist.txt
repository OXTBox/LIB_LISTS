 This pale, pathetic outcast was abducted as a boy by kobolds. For more than a decade he was their slave, part of a crew forced to stoke and repair a massive techno-magical engine deep within the earth, This ordeal left him half-mad, hideously burned... and with a very unusual skill set. Can pick locks, disarm traps, and otherwise tinker with complex machines at 70%. Understands the basics of some very non-Medieval concepts like steam power, hydraulics, etc.
