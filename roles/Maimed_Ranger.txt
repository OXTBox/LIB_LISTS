 An elf wrapped in covering headgear sojourns at the Keep for a few days. He is hunting the bandits from wilderness area 3, though he does not yet know where they are based. He is actively recruiting help in the form of adventurers, but as he has nothing to pay them with, he appeals to their moral character and service to the greater good of the Keep. People befriending this taciturn elf may be offered a glimpse under his headwraps - both ears have been savagely cut off, and no amount of magic has been able to restore them; this was his 'prize' for being captured 3 months ago by a group of the bandits - the rest of his party was slain. He claims the bandits have some kind of evil power behind their group, as one of them laid a curse upon him after his ears were removed (preventing the healing)
