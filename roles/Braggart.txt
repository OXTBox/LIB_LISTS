 A massive man equipped with a variety of magical items, including a helmet made from a dragon's skull. He will loudly and at length speak of his great accomplishments and future ambitions, in exchange for a round of drinks or chunk of meat of course. They are all lies - in truth, the man was a miner who stumbled upon an ancient tomb filled with the items he now wears. While he thought it was odd that such a treasure was unguarded, he never truly questioned his luck, as he was unaware that there was an intelligent undead guardian not far away. It was prevented from killing him then as the doorway had been sealed off by a collapse some years before, but now that it has dug its way out it hunts the thief at every turn. The undead is well preserved enough that it can pass as a (sickly) living man, at least so long as it wears its cloak, and intelligent enough to gather information and hire proxies to do its dirty work
