 Half man, half orc. Born at the Keep to a pilgrim girl who was raped, he has played the role of "Bad cop" for multiple Castellans, both Lawful and Chaotic. Spends most of his life in the Keep's dungeons, but he comes out to drink, talk religion, and play chess on moonless nights, or when the Castellan wants to subtly strike fear into his subordinates. The Torturer never raises his voice or threatens anyone. He lets his hulking frame and black, boar-like eyes do his intimidating for him. The people of the Keep regard him with fear to his face, and disgust to his back... only the wise Curate treats him as an equal, and The Torturer repays that respect.
