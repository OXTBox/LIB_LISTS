 A young girl wearing an odd outfit of fur and carved wood and bone. She is seeking her long lost parents, and her latest information has lead her to the keep. While the girl is sweet and charming, her presence is provoking a great deal of anxiety at the keep because she rarely strays far from her gigantic wolf companion. The wolf is herself very intelligent and capable of speech, but does not do so at the keep as to avoid inflaming their fear further. The wolf is genuinely devoted to aiding the rider find her parents, and may speak to those of good hearts and open minds who are willing to aide their quest. In return, she can offer her knowledge of the wilderness and many secret things hidden therein
