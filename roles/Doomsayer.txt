 A kindly but dour-countenanced portly fellow arrives in the Keep and begins to start preaching in the market area of the doom that awaits mankind at the hands of wicked demi-humans and their evil gods. All of this has been brought about by man's apathy towards their goodly deities and the sinful practices of alcohol and promiscuous sexual appetites. Normally, this kind of behavior wouldn't be tolerated inside the keep for more than a few minutes, but the cleric is not only genuine in his beliefs, but he has healed several people that the current clergy of the chapel could not/would not heal. He has also made a rather substantial donation to the coffers of the Keep upon arrival, so he is allowed to preach his message ... for now. He could be a cultist/infiltrator, a legitimate challenge to the current clergy, or a diversion for other activities
