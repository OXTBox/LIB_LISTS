 This elderly gentleman with perfect manners and a morbid wit has lived at the Keep his entire life, serving under multiple Castellans and flags. In his time he has preformed every type of hanging imaginable. Mass hangings, hangings of children and infants, hangings of those already dead, and even of beings with more than one neck. Loves the Keep itself, but is neutral in regards to whatever nation or power currently controls it. Recently has adopted a 9-year-old orphan boy and is training him to take his place, teaching him the unwritten arts of rope-boiling, rope-soaping, and making the perfect knot. A consummate professional who prides himself on always giving "A quick death and a good, clean show"
