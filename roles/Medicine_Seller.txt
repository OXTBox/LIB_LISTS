 Or at least that is what this tattooed elf claims to be. While always well mannered and polite, his behavior is odd enough to be downright unnerving, particularly the way he is always calm, collected, and smirking regardless of the situation. In addition to medicine he sells a variety of odd, and even a few scandalous, objects. In truth he is a wandering exorcist, and he subtly displays interest in anything supernatural
